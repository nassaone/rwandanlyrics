RwandanLyrics takes copyright concerns seriously. If you are a musician, songwriter, publisher, record label, or authorized representative and believe that lyrics or other material on our website infringes your copyright, you may submit a removal request.

## How to Request Removal

Send an email to <%COPYRIGHT_EMAIL%> with the subject line:

**Copyright Takedown Request – RwandanLyrics**

Please include:

1. Your name and contact details.
2. The title of the song or creative work concerned.
3. The URL of each page you want us to review.
4. Evidence or a reasonable explanation of your copyright ownership or authority to represent the rights holder.
5. An explanation of why the material is unauthorized.
6. A statement confirming that the information in your request is accurate to the best of your knowledge.

You may attach relevant documentation if available. Please do not send unnecessary sensitive personal information.

## What Happens Next?

After receiving your request, we will:

1. Review the complaint and determine whether additional information is needed.
2. Assess the reported content and any supporting evidence.
3. Where appropriate, restrict access to or remove the material while the matter is reviewed.
4. Contact you if clarification is required.
5. Inform you of the outcome where reasonably possible.

We aim to acknowledge complete requests within five business days. This is a target, not a guarantee, and urgent cases may be prioritized. More complex complaints may take longer.

## Requests from Authorized Representatives

If you are acting for an artist, publisher, label, or another rights holder, please explain your authority to submit the request on their behalf.

## Incorrect or Disputed Removals

If you believe content was removed in error, or you have permission or a license to publish it, contact us at <%COPYRIGHT_EMAIL%> with relevant evidence. We will review the matter before deciding whether the content should be restored.

## Important Notice

Please submit only genuine, good-faith complaints. False or misleading claims may cause harm and may have legal consequences under applicable law.

Submitting a request does not guarantee removal, and this process does not replace any formal legal notice or procedure required by applicable law.
